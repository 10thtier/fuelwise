Help and support

Questions, problems or a product that looks wrong? Email fuelwise.app@outlook.com.

Product details come from Open Food Facts, a free database built by volunteers. If a listing is wrong, you can fix it from the product page in the app.

Privacy

Your profile and health details stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Not medical advice

Fuelwise helps you choose food. It isn’t medical advice and doesn’t diagnose or treat anything.
